Problem Statement & Root Cause

The "Aaple Sarkar" Crisis: Why Legacy Portals Fail

Aaple Sarkar digitized paper forms into web forms, but preserved the corrupt manual bottleneck.

🔁
1. The 14-Time Upload Trap
A farmer must upload the same 7/12 extract, Aadhaar, and salary slip 14 times a year for 14 different certificates and schemes.
⏳
2. Artificial Rent-Seeking Delays
Clerks intentionally sit on files for 21 to 45 days until touts collect 'speed money' (लाच) to advance files to the Nayab Tahsildar.
📄
3. Rampant Photocopy Forgery
Physical ink stamps and forged seals are easily photoshopped. Colleges (CAP rounds) and MPSC cannot verify authenticity offline.
The Core Failure: Current portals automate the filing of forms, but automate zero percent of the statutory evaluation or dispatch decisions.

Citizen Data Sovereignty

MahaVault: Sovereign "Once-Only" Document Locker

Compliant with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 & Zero-PII principles.

Cryptographic Security Flow

1. Zero-PII Salting & Hashing:
Aadhaar & PAN are never stored in plaintext. They are salted and stored as SHA-256 digests.
2. Envelope Encryption (AES-256-GCM):
Each document is encrypted with a unique Data Encryption Key (DEK). The DEK is encrypted under a state Key Encryption Key (KEK).
3. Sovereign Auto-Fetch:
Applying for a new certificate takes 1 click; the system pulls pre-verified Form 16, 7/12, and SLC automatically.
